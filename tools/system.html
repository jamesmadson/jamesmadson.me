Motion

$spring-lift linear(0, 0.55 28%, 1.04 62%, 0.99 82%, 1) — transform only, one soft overshoot. Entrances rise 20px over 0.8s with one overshoot; exits and collapses stay plain. Color feedback lives on the 150ms lane. Hover these to feel the contract:

The About pill contract: no border any state, 7% white frost at rest, project flood on hover, mini-label floored at 11px, 44px touch floor under hover:none, reduced-motion keeps only an opacity fade.

Motion principles

Frequency of encounterMotion budget
Rare (a visitor sees it once)Expressive welcome: the hero film, the fog settle
Occasional (per visit)Subtle and fast: entrances, the carousel
Frequent (hovers, nav)Minimal: 150ms color, one small spring
Keyboard-initiatedNever animates

The standing rules: animate transform and opacity only; transitions over keyframes for interactive states so motion stays interruptible; one soft overshoot per entrance, exits plain and slightly faster; stagger delays live with the JS or the visible-state selector, never the base rule (delays on the base run the stagger backwards on the way out); ambient motion yields the moment the user shows intent (the course map stops cycling on first click); anything continuously animating is a battery decision as much as a design one. prefers-reduced-motion is a hard gate: no autoplay, no auto-cycle, no transform motion, posters still communicate.

Materials · the fog

card fog

ambient art under a 62→86% veil of the surface's own color, blur 34px

hero fog

same recipe at blur 64px with a dark veil, masked to feather at the edges

One material, three surfaces (home hero, case heroes, work cards). The veil always carries the surface's own color so self-theming and ink contrast hold. New fog accents must join the preload list or the first swap pops.
