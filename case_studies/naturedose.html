Hundreds of studies link time in nature to lower stress, better sleep, and faster recovery. But no one had made it easy to track, set goals around, or actually feel progress in. NatureDose® was built to change that.

I lead design for NatureQuant, responsible for every touchpoint from brand identity to production screens. On NatureDose I owned the original concept, art direction, and branding, designed the core UX screens and App Store screens, and prototyped the app as it came together. A development partner carried it the rest of the way, working through error states and the edge cases that only surface once something is real.

Challenges

Nature is a well-researched health intervention that had never been treated like one. The job was to put time outside alongside diet, exercise, and sleep as something you can measure. I was the only designer: brand, UX, visual design, and the design system.

The design challenges were:

  • Turn peer-reviewed research into something that reads on a phone in five seconds.

  • Motivate people outdoors without nagging them.

  • Hold the experience together across iOS, Android, and web.

Design

Every prototype tested the same question: how much data can you show before it stops motivating anyone?

What shipped: passive tracking, a daily goal, and a progress ring you check without thinking about it.

Impact

NatureDose is backed by NatureQuant's research and a partnership with Outside Interactive.

People set a goal, track sessions automatically, and see where they stand. NatureScore® tells them not just how long they were outside, but whether it counted.
