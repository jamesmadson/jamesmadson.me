Case Study Redesigning St. Jude Cloud's Pediatric Cancer Knowledge Base

Overview

Client

St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital

Role

Lead Product Designer

Website

pecan.stjude.cloud

Contributions

  • UI/UX Design
  • Design Systems
  • Data Visualization
  • Front-End Development
  • Strategic Communication

I led the redesign of PeCan (Pediatric Cancer Knowledge Base), St. Jude Cloud's knowledge base for pediatric cancer genomics which collects and organizes data from thousands of pediatric cancer genomes, connecting scientists to research findings around the world.

PeCan is one of St. Jude Cloud’s cornerstone research tools, but over time it became limited by it’s interface, growing apart from the rest of the ecosystem. The challenge was to redesign PeCan so it could provide richer insights without having to write code, while matching the quality of its data: clear, consistent, and accessible for scientists and collaborators everywhere.

The work spanned brand identity, UI/UX design, front-end development, copywriting, and launch strategy. This transformed PeCan into a more approachable, unified experience within the St. Jude Cloud ecosystem. I helped define key product features, built the updated design system integration, and wrote front-end code and supporting materials for launch, newsletters, and documentation.

The result: a clearer, faster, and more human platform for researchers exploring genetic insights into childhood cancer.

Design

We replaced dense tables and long lists with flexible data views that help users uncover relationships between datasets, diagnoses, and publications. Interactive elements guide researchers through complex results without overwhelming them.

The new interface emphasizes clarity and readability through structured grids, soft neutrals, and gentle gradients—balancing scientific precision with approachability. Each page was restructured for improved navigation and user focus.

Impact

The redesign helped PeCan stand out as a modern research product while remaining cohesive with the rest of the St. Jude Cloud ecosystem. It now serves as an accessible, reliable hub for pediatric cancer data—used by researchers across St. Jude and partner institutions worldwide.

  • Stronger visual and UX alignment across all St. Jude Cloud tools

  • Consistent tone and messaging across marketing, product, and documentation

  • Faster time-to-insight for researchers exploring genomic data

  • AACR (American Association for Cancer Research) presentation of software and new features

  • Clearer communication of findings and publications

  • Cross-department collaboration

PeCan’s redesign strengthened St. Jude’s broader mission: to share knowledge openly and accelerate cures for children everywhere.

AACR poster design to present new features and findings

Reflection

This project reinforced how design, language, and strategy work best when they serve the same goal: helping people connect with complex information easily.

Redesigning PeCan wasn’t just about improving a research tool—it was about bringing clarity and consistency to something that saves lives. Finding cures. Saving children.
