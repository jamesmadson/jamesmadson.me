Overview

Product design engineer with 15+ years across startups, research institutions, and independent practice. I care most about work that actually helps people, whether that means redesigning a complex data tool, building a brand from nothing, or writing code myself when it gets to that point. I lead by supporting: mentoring designers, and setting direction through systems, from design systems to the skills and digital personas that let teams test ideas fast with AI. Comfortable leading, comfortable contributing, genuinely uncomfortable with work that doesn't hold up.

Lead Product Designer

St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, St. Jude Cloud 2019 – 2025 · Remote, Memphis TN

St. Jude Cloud is a global genomics data platform, home to petabytes of childhood cancer research data made accessible to researchers and clinicians worldwide. I was the lead designer for six years, covering everything from day-to-day feature work to major platform overhauls.

Drove the full redesign of prominent research tool, PeCan, introduced a design system used across eight+ applications, and shipped a series of zero-to-one products that are now actively used by the research community. Worked directly with PMs, engineers, and research scientists, often as one of the first to define what we were trying to build.

  • Led end-to-end redesign of PeCan, a pediatric cancer knowledge base serving researchers at 300+ institutions
  • Built and maintained the platform design system, spanning components, patterns, and documentation
  • Designed the Visualization Community from the ground up, a tool for creating and sharing interactive genomics research
  • Managed a small design team; ran critique, mentored junior designers, and kept things moving without a lot of overhead

Design Director

NatureQuant 2019 – 2024 · Remote

Joined the founders as the lead and only designer, which meant owning everything. Brand identity, marketing site (designed and built a large part of it), pitch decks, whitepapers, email templates, client-facing materials, product concepts, and prototypes. Learned geospatial mapping tools specifically for this work. The scientific credibility was there from day one; my job was to make everything feel as serious as the research behind it.

NatureDose was a mobile wellness app that helped people track and build nature habits against a research-backed dose target. NatureScore was a location-based index, essentially a walkability score for nature exposure. Both shipped. Both worked. I'm proud of the brand as much as the product work; we made something that looked genuinely different in a crowded wellness market.

  • Created the full visual identity and brand language for NatureQuant from zero
  • Designed and shipped NatureDose on iOS and Android, from concept through App Store launch
  • Designed NatureScore, a data-dense consumer product that had to feel approachable
  • Covered the full scope: product, brand, marketing, pitch materials, client consulting, and art direction
  • Built Juniper, the NatureQuant design system, used across all product and marketing surfaces
  • Defined UX strategy and product roadmap alongside the CEO and engineering leads
