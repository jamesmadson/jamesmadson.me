About

GitHub ↗ Resume

I've never fit neatly into one label. UI designer, web master, front-end developer, and half a dozen others along the way, until design engineer came along. I thrive in ambiguity and bring distilled clarity to whatever I'm working on.

I led design end-to-end for NatureDose, from zero to a 4.6★ App Store rating. I built PeCan for researchers at St. Jude, and shipped visualization tools now cited in academic papers. I care about the craft, but I care more about whether it worked.

I lead by supporting, not directing. I mentored junior designers at St. Jude and kept a small team moving without much overhead. Today that also means leading through systems: design systems for people, and skills and digital personas that let teams test ideas fast with AI.

Things I've always cared about and tried to bring to every project. If the work isn't useful, nothing else really matters, but if it's also clear and built to last, that's when it starts to feel right.

I love solving a real need with something beautiful. Working with a great team to get there makes it better every time. I try to think big, move fast, and care about the details, always with an eye toward actually helping the people using it.
